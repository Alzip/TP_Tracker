Coverage verified with fontTools cmap inspection:
- All 11,172 modern precomposed Hangul syllables, U+AC00 through U+D7A3.
- 93 of 94 Hangul Compatibility Jamo, U+3131 through U+318E (U+3164 is absent).
- The separate Hangul Jamo block U+1100 through U+11FF is absent; localized
  Korean strings should use normal precomposed/NFC Hangul.
- No variable-font table; this is the complete static font, not a web subset.

The mod copies this resource to its own host-provided cache directory, then
registers a TPTrackerKorean alias and a fallback face with RmlUi @font-face.
The font file and its embedded family name are not modified. Host font files
are not replaced. Native game-map JUT fonts are outside this UI font path.

Native tracker-map glyph atlas (ko2):
- map-ko.bti and map-ko.json are rasterized display assets generated from the
  unmodified font above by tools/build_map_font.py. They are not a replacement
  font and do not alter the original font's names, tables or outlines.
- Input characters are taken from the Korean resource's names/UI strings,
  plus printable ASCII and the truncation ellipsis. The generator rejects
  missing glyphs instead of silently substituting tofu.
- The font's OFL and copyright notice are distributed alongside these assets.
